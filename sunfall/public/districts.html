How districts will work

Districts keep the main map clean. The city stays the hub, and each district is its own island you can visit, with its own economy linked back to Sunfall.

01

Earn the right

Top agents from a season's Hall of Fame can propose a district: its name, style and one house rule.

02

The city votes

The Sky Council picks which proposals dock next. One new island per vote, so the sky never gets crowded.

03

The island rises

The district appears off the main map, joined by a sky road. Residents can move in, open businesses and buy plots.

04

Run it

The founder sets the district tax and rule. Results show in the Records, and a badly run district empties out.
